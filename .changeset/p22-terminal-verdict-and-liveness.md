**A round is judged by its terminal verdict, and its liveness is read from the child alone.** Every dispatched round now closes with a verdict — `{ treeClean, targetAction }` — written into its own round record, and the record itself is unconditional: a blocked or timed-out round books a record too (`op`, cycle, attempt, the pre-dispatch HEAD as `dispatchBase`, the verdict, the graded violations and the carrier path), so "the round did nothing" is never a silent absence. Completion is a criterion per verb, never the child's exit code: `implement` requires a commit since the group's baseline (the earliest round record's `dispatchBase`), `review` requires a findings carrier that exists and validates (`{"findings": []}` is an approving carrier, not a violation), and `fix` requires a disposition for every finding of the requesting review handoff. Hard violations (an empty round, a missing or malformed judge carrier, an unmet disposition criterion, a missing or contract-violating test-evidence file, a plan structure edit made inside the round) exit non-zero and are booked; soft ones (an absent implement carrier, a dirty tree) only warn and join the derived `contractViolationCount`. The persisted counter retires — the count derives from the records, the tree observation rides `verdict.treeClean`, and `completedCycles` answers "did this round succeed" as a verdict rather than a membership test.

The fix disposition slot is new on the work carrier: a map keyed by the requesting finding's `summary` text verbatim, valued `{ status: "fixed" | "rejected", reason?: string }`, with six named hard cases around it. The requesting handoff stays read-only apart from a ruling appended to its `notes`, and the docs-fix shell carries the same obligation as the work-fix shell.

Each round also gets its own log: the three dispatch verbs run the child over pipes and land its stdout and stderr in `.kairos/cdd/<slug>/logs/<stem>.log` (the report stem, `-a{attempt}` on a re-dispatch, created before the spawn, drained and closed after the exit, never echoed). The round's own `CDD_BLOCKED` line — rejected, cut short, timed out or hard-judged — now names that path; a pre-flight refusal (dirty tree · in-flight · doc contract) names none, because no round was ever dispatched. Liveness loses its workspace-mtime leg: the sampler reads two child-owned signals instead (log growth and process-group CPU advance), any one advancing leg counts as progress, and the four states are unchanged — including `stalled` after the idle threshold when a single leg is readable and `unknown` (fail-open) only when no leg is readable at all. A wave carries at most three tasks, enforced as a hard gate in two places — the plan-scope rule `wave-width-at-most-three` and the wave gate's `wave-too-wide` / `too-many-tasks` refusals, both naming the offending task ids and the `DependsOn` repair. The engine never splits, truncates or reorders a wave.

"Next" converges to one derivation: `NextStepRouter#nextHop` answers the face, the in-face hop, the face-to-face station and the rendering route in one object, read by the capsule, the dispatch gate, the board and the lifecycle frontier alike — while the loop-versus-converge judgment stays exactly where it was, in the blocker/warn/nit count table. The board renders per verb: `review 10 ✓ · fix 10 ✓`, a `✗` mark that cannot be omitted, findings as a `+N` suffix (omitted at zero), an attempt superscript only above the first attempt, and no range form — there is no `--verbose` flag. The round record's plan-structure snapshot judges a structural plan edit made inside a round as a hard violation, presented only through that round's `violations`.

The package README (both languages) now states the round log path, the two wave-width refusals and their repair, the `dispositions` slot and the review-cycle soft cap (by constant name — no copied number). `cdd implement --help` states the wave width alone, in its `description` and on the `--tasks` options line.
