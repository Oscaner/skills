The `doc-contract` gate now refuses the claims a document cannot verify. Six rule rows land in `contract/rules.ts#RULE_REGISTRY` — `measure-literal` · `line-coordinate` · `live-arithmetic` · `temporal-indicator` (the four variable classes) · `single-home` (one criterion, one home) · `change-history-date-shape` — and they ship at `block`, so a hit refuses the round instead of annotating it. The `checked:` face gains the tiered words (`warned — <n> warning(s)`) and the two skips (`skipped (closed document)` · `skipped (no doc target)`), the refusal gains one `details` row naming a missing closure registration, findings gain the `evidence` key (`CDD_BLOCKED: evidence-missing:` is the one new refusal message), and the rulings sidecar gains `verdict` / `probe`. The claim-shape vocabulary the spec review rubric and the two authoring skills share now states a count only as `census:<id> = <n>`, the id taken from the census registry — naming the guard that maintains the value is the reason for the reference, not the form — and `cdd-plan`'s `author-plan` node carries the same three lines `cdd-spec-writer`'s `author-spec` node does. Closed documents are exempt from the six shape rows; every other rule keeps judging them.
