Takeover becomes an authorization of two facts, and the `single-home` rule retires (P44).

`--recover` still never crosses `alive === true`: a live holder is refused, and a vanished
target is never grounds for an automatic release. What changed is the authority behind the
flag. It used to ride a structure leg the dispatch could skip; it is now the **conjunction**
of two readings — the `in-flight:` row present in the seam's `checked:` face (the in-flight
gate ran and passed) *and* a non-null `InFlight.staleState()` (the record is genuinely stale).
Neither reading stands alone: the row is pushed before the refusal it may itself carry, and
`staleState()` says nothing about the tree-clean gate that returns ahead of it. The judgment
therefore runs whenever the derived flag is set, refusal or not, and it no longer steals the
first refuser's verdict — a structure refusal with no landed leg returns `allowed`, so
`CDD_BLOCKED: in-flight` (with the holder's pid and command) appears only for a live holder,
`CDD_BLOCKED: tree-clean` for a dirty tree under the flag, and a document-refused tree under
the flag takes the record over, clears it, and books exactly one `interrupted` abort record
with no second dispatch. The in-flight gate itself stays judge-only: `acquire` and
`foldAbort` keep the writes, `session/preflight.ts` gains none.

A held record now judges its **own target facts** — the plan it names and the workspace
directory of its slug — as two cells on the held branch, with no third boolean: "the target
is gone" is the derived fold of the cells, stored nowhere, because a folded cell could not
say which fact read out missing. The fourth state renders through one row
(`CAPSULE_WORDS.inFlight.targetGone`) with only the cells that read out missing, and one
choice of takeover hint serves its four readers — the `in-flight:` checked row, the blocking
report, the board row and the dry-run hint. A record with a live pid renders the held bytes
whatever its cells read, and `stealable` keeps its one meaning ("the holder is gone, the
target still stands").

`single-home` retires with its machinery: the `SingleHomeInvariant`, the sentence index (the
length floor, the Jaccard threshold, the work-item exemption, the 3-gram comparison) and its
fixture leave the rule plane, so the shape gate's roster is `line-coordinate` ·
`live-arithmetic` · `change-history-date-shape` and the registry reads 31 doc-scope rows + 2
ledger-scope rows. A word-boundary scan of the `contract/` and `face/` source planes keeps the
retired id and its prose spellings at zero, and the selector-coverage neighbourhood plant
moves to `CrossDocChainInvariant#specChain` (a carrier whose `program:` names a planted
overall outside its `parent-version` lineage) rather than a second sweep.

Two prompt-plane bytes ride along, both renderings of text already ruled: the
`spec-consistency` lens row asks a reading **which items it counts and whether those items are
still on the tree** (the reading/referent drift is that question's answer, never a new
criterion), the shared `spec-lens` row gains S4 ("looks implemented, the implementation looks
wrong"), the calibration tail gains S5 (report every axis separately, never one "most severe"
finding), and the new `cl:own-document` discipline clause bounds a round's write authority to
its own document set — `TEMPLATE_PROMPT.$version` 26 is their one mechanical signal. Zero new
lens labels, zero new tiers, zero new gate names: the lens row and cell counts are untouched.
