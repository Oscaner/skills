Read the plan and the document identities through the index, and judge ownership through the matrix. `PlanSource` now takes the command's ONE `SlugIndex` and asks it both questions: the plan path is the index's plan row `rel` (the `<slug>.md` / `<slug>-design.md` derivations are gone from the engine), a clean-miss surface answers `undeclared` / `spec-absent`, and a surface that dropped or failed to register candidates answers an undecided read — today's board bytes plus one counted `CDD_WARN:` line, zero refusal. A slug naming two plans answers the set (`MARK_SLUG_DUPLICATED` plus every `rel`, no `kairos:cdd-plan` hop) instead of "no plan". `SlugIndex#discarded()` exposes the per-root dropped/unregistered counts, and `cross-doc-chain` reads the same counts so a plan whose identity document merely could not be read is no longer refused. `CarrierSeal.sweep` takes the matrix (required, nullable: no matrix seals nothing) and follows its `sealed` column only, `RoundNaming.isCarrierName` and the ledger's two carrier patterns are retired, `Ledger#orphanHandoffs` becomes the two-tier predicate over the whole workspace listing (`attribute → null`, or a round-domain carrier no record claims), `Workspace#names()` is the one directory read, and the round/workspace read face reports a same-identity variant beside the rendered name as `misplaced`.
